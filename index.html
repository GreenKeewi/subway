      How service grades work

      A perfect stop is within 3m of the orange marker. Gold service means at least 80% perfect stops, 85% comfort and no safety interventions. Normal service braking is always comfortable; emergency braking and sustained overspeed reduce comfort. Best scores are saved separately for each route and manual or assisted driving.
